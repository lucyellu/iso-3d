Lucy Lu is a Canadian 3D animator and developer. She animated on the Netflix series Beat Bugs and Nick Jr.'s Little Charmers, then founded Paperbox Studio and shipped games to the App Store. She also works on number theory, writes essays on science and history, and builds web apps, games and simulations, most of which run in a browser.1

Education

Lu studied at the University of Alberta and then trained in 3D animation at Vancouver Film School.1

Animation

Lu animated at Atomic Cartoons in Vancouver. Her credits include Beat Bugs, a Netflix series built around Beatles songs, and Little Charmers for Nick Jr. and Treehouse TV.1 She later worked freelance; her reel is on Vimeo.

Games and apps

As Paperbox Studio, Lu released three games for iPhone and iPad: Lunescape, Starbreaker and Math Gym. They are no longer listed on the App Store, but gameplay of Starbreaker and Lunescape is on her apps page.1

She founded Neuralens, a computer-vision checkout system that identifies and prices a tray of items from a single photo. Other projects include an interactive 3D periodic table and Booklit, an ebook reader with read-aloud.

Mathematics and writing

With Suqin Ao, Lu wrote On the Prime Pairs, which uses a modified Sieve of Eratosthenes to connect the Twin Prime Conjecture with Goldbach's conjecture. The paper is published with source code and a runnable notebook.1

Her essays include a reading of the Rosetta Stone as engineering and a look at the Geminid meteor shower as a yearly delivery of material from space that is barely measured. She also built a reading-level slider that rewrites an article for different readers.

Recent work

Main article: List of works by Lucy Lu

Since 2026 Lu has built with AI coding agents, running several projects in parallel. In September 2026 alone she made 181 commits across 17 projects.2

Impossible Waterway

Her homepage is an Escher-style isometric waterway built in Three.js. A boat carries her CV around a loop that can only exist from one viewing angle.1 It launched at lucylu.org on 30 September 2026. Source.

Fly Dome and Fly Sim

Two linked simulations that drive an embodied fruit fly with published connectome data, the wiring diagram of a real fly brain. Each fly lives under a Victorian glass bell dome, with Sims-style needs for hunger, grooming and sleep.3

myspot

A music player for her own catalogue that became a 24/7 radio station with neural-voice DJs in September 2026.4 Listen · source.

Other projects

NoteForge Piano turns a song into a piano roll, sheet music and chords in the browser. Paper Sim folds paper and packaging in 3D. Atlas Library maps the size, mass and temperature of things from electrons to galaxies. Jelly Toss, made with a collaborator, is a physics game about throwing jelly candies onto plates.

Music

Lu writes songs with Suno and has a catalogue of more than 4,000 titles.5 They play on her radio station, myspot.

Selected projects

All of these open in a browser. The apps page has the full list with videos.

  • Snark Weather: a weather app with opinions.
  • Breathe: a guided timer for breath holds.
  • Stockwave: fast-forward paper trading on real market data.
  • NoteForge Piano: any song as a falling-note piano roll.
  • Atlas Library: the scale of everything, from electrons to galaxies.
  • Paper Sim: fold paper and boxes in 3D, then flatten them back to the pattern.
  • Milk & Letters: a cozy word game in a bowl of cereal.
  • Earth's Sensorium: 124 live instruments that measure the planet.
