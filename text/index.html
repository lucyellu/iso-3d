Lucy Lu

Plain version of lucylu.org, which is an interactive 3D scene.

I'm a 3D animator and software developer in Metro Vancouver. I animated on Beat Bugs (Netflix) and Little Charmers (Nick Jr., Treehouse TV), ran Paperbox Studio, a design studio that also released three iPad games, and now build web applications and interactive 3D. I studied biology at the University of Alberta and 3D animation at Vancouver Film School.

My CV has the details.

Things I've made

Everything here runs in a browser. The full list, with videos, is at lucylu.org/apps.

  • 23andsee: compares raw 23andMe genome exports entirely offline, in the browser. Nothing leaves your machine.
  • OSKM Explorer: the four Yamanaka reprogramming factors in 3D.
  • Earth's Sensorium: 124 live instruments that measure the planet.
  • Paper Sim: fold paper and boxes in 3D, then flatten them back to the pattern.
  • Atlas Library: the scale of everything, from electrons to galaxies.
  • Starbreaker and Lunescape: Paperbox Studio's retired iPad games, with gameplay video.
