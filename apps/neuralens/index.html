OUR TECHNOLOGY

01 / FAST

We create smart self-checkout systems powered by AI. Our models are trained on real data, and can detect and identify products faster than humans.

Our tools help reduce labour and training costs by taking on some of the cognitive load traditionally done by human staff.

02 / ACCURATE

We understand that accuracy is extremely important to any business. Our predictions get better and better with increased usage and data.

Neuralens' proprietary POS system handles documentation and verification in case of discrepancies.

03 / SIMPLE

We get that not every business knows how to start working with AI-powered tools, even if they want to.

That's why all of our products are created with regular people in mind. You don't need to be a data scientist or software engineer to get started.
