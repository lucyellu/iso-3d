Atlas of Mass

Forty-four things, from the lightest known particle to all the matter in the observable universe, plus five limits and constants. Mass cannot be drawn to scale, so each sits on a logarithmic beam where every long tick is ten times heavier. Or put any two on the scales.

Masses are typical measured values; where nature varies or the figure is an estimate, the note says so (“~” marks approximate values, “<” an upper limit). Italic entries are limits, units and masses set by constants rather than objects. Constant values are CODATA 2022. Drawings are not to scale. “Same mass as a cube of water” uses 1 g per cm³. The coloured band under the beam shows what gravity does to a lump of that much matter, from shapeless rock to round worlds, brown dwarfs, stars coloured by mass, and clusters; hover it for the thresholds, which are approximate.
