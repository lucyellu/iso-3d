Italic entries and dashed ruler ticks are limits, scales and lengths set by constants rather than objects. Constant values are CODATA 2022. The coloured band under the ruler is the electromagnetic spectrum by wavelength; hover it for band edges. Sizes are typical measured values; where nature varies, the note gives the range.
